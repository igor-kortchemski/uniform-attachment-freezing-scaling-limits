# Coverage gaps for inventory items without a report row

Scope: the [source inventory](source-inventory.md) items that have no row in
[FORMALIZATION_REPORT.md](../FORMALIZATION_REPORT.md), namely U01, U04 (split
in the inventory into U04a and U04b), U05, U07, U09, U10, U22, U23, U33 and
U42–U49, together with the external dependencies E01–E11. U20 is handled
separately and is not reviewed here. Several of these items have since received
their own report rows (U01, U04b, U23, U49 and E09); the table keeps them so
that every item still has one classification.

Status date: rows revised on 30 October 2026 to the state of the latest
all-source check (`evidence/gp-lemmas-axioms.log`: 3,524 declarations in all
283 modules). For each revised row, every cited declaration was found by grep
in `Freezing2/`, and its module lies in the import closure of `Freezing2.lean`.

Method: the original mapping is a read-only audit made on 29 October 2026. For each item, the
inventory entry and the cited lines of `attachement-uniforme-2-final.tex` were
read. `Freezing2/**/*.lean` was then searched by name and docstring, including
the vendored `Freezing2/Companion/` (see [companion provenance](companion-provenance.md)).
For the M05 items, the pinned Mathlib under `.lake/packages/mathlib/Mathlib`
and the read-only companion project `../freezing1` were also searched.
No `lake` command was run. "Covered" means that a maintained declaration
exists whose statement matches the claim. Build and axiom status rely on the
report's latest all-source check, which was not rerun here.

Classification key: **(a)** covered; **(b)** partially covered; **(c)** not
covered; **(d)** purely external or background, not a source claim that needs
a proof here.

## External mathematics missing for M05 (pinned Mathlib v4.35.0)

- **Brownian motion.** `Mathlib/Probability/BrownianMotion/Basic.lean` defines only the predicates `IsPreBrownianReal` and `IsBrownianReal`: finite-dimensional laws equal to the Gaussian projective family, plus continuous paths. No existence theorem is proved. Kolmogorov extension is available only as uniqueness (`IsProjectiveLimit.unique`). `Probability/Process/Kolmogorov.lean` defines `IsKolmogorovProcess`, but the Kolmogorov–Chentsov theorem itself is not proved.
- **Brownian excursion and its invariance principles.** There is no excursion, bridge or meander. There is no Donsker or Kaigh invariance principle, no Skorokhod space $D[0,1]$ or $J_1$ topology, and no Skorokhod representation theorem.
- **Trees.** Mathlib has no real trees, Brownian CRT, contour or height coding, or plane-tree type. The project defines its own `IsRealTree` in `Geometry/RealTree.lean`.
- **Discrete tools.** Mathlib has `DyckWord`, `catalan`, Stirling's formula and central-binomial bounds. It has no ballot theorem and no local limit theorem for simple random walk.
- **Internal gaps for M05.** (1) The mixture identity at lines 393–397 needs $f\mapsto$ `criticalTreeLaw (1/f)` to be measurable; nothing proves this, since the law is defined by `Classical.epsilon`. (2) M03 is stated for deterministic arrays with strict survival, so the CRT array violates it at its horizon (S14).
- **Companion Theorem 2.** `../freezing1` proves `thm:BGW` (row T02), but its uniform-plane-tree example (row A03) is open, and none of this is vendored here.

## Summary

**Covered since the original mapping** (see the revised rows): U01 first clause; U04b supercritical non-tightness and the S19-qualified recursive-tree clause; U23 in law; U49 and E09, including the Polish GP space and S18 as a projection; E10 through N15 and N16; Lean refutations of S15 and S16 for U44, U45 and E08.

**Tractable uncovered pieces** (rough Lean sizes from the original mapping; none blocked by missing external probability):

- E02 and U01 second clause, conditioned uniform plane tree: about 800–2,000 lines by direct counting, or vendoring the 121-module T02 closure from `../freezing1` (about 17,500 lines) plus about 300–600 lines of conditioning.
- U04a, dimension lower bounds: large, about 2,000–4,000 lines. Minkowski dimension, packing dimension and the Frostman or energy lemma must all be built first, plus a two-point small-ball estimate. The source gives no proof.
- U43, deterministic part: about 300–800 lines. Uniform convergence of the scaled walk implies the $L^2$ and $O(\sqrt n)$ hypotheses; uniform integrability implies $L^1$ through Mathlib's Vitali theorem. Handling the terminal time of S14 is medium-sized.
- U44, a valid replacement estimate with $C=C(\varepsilon)$ through exact ballot counts and Stirling's formula (E08, discrete): about 1,000–2,000 lines.
- E05, the Skorokhod representation theorem on Polish spaces: about 1,500–3,000 lines. It is standard but absent from Mathlib, and it is needed only by the CRT section.
- E04 and U24, the stochastic time change to Aldous's coalescent: not estimated. Aldous's Theorem 2, the other part of E04, needs the CRT and is blocked (below).

**Blocked by external mathematics:**

- M05, U42, U46, U47 and U48, and E07, together with the probabilistic halves of U43 and U44. All need a constructed Brownian excursion (Mathlib has no existence of Brownian motion, no excursion and no Kolmogorov–Chentsov theorem) and a Brownian CRT law.
- They also need Kaigh's invariance principle, Aldous's plane-tree-to-CRT theorem and Skorokhod representation, all absent.
- M05 and U48 additionally need measurability of $f\mapsto$ `criticalTreeLaw (1/f)`, a terminal-horizon version of M03 (S14), and, for U48, a pointed limit tree.
- Aldous's Theorem 2 (part of E04), an alternative route to M05, likewise needs the CRT. E04 is bypassed for M03, and its density part is discharged by N11.
